About

I am an Assistant Professor at the Gran Sasso Science Institute in L'Aquila, Italy, and I work in neutron star astrophysics and multimessenger astronomy. My research connects the long-term physics inside neutron stars, such as how their magnetic fields and heat evolve, with the transient signals we observe when these objects merge or flare.

I am a member of the LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA (LVK) Collaboration and co-developed MATINS, a three-dimensional code that follows the coupled magnetic and thermal evolution of neutron stars.

I completed my PhD in December 2018 and spent three years at the Institute of Space Sciences (ICE-CSIC) in Barcelona before moving to GSSI. In 2025 I obtained the Italian national qualification (ASN) for Associate Professor.
